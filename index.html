01 / About

Built for the curious.

A place to explore ideas without forcing them into a single category.

“The world is too interesting to be ordinary.”

ZebraTuxedo exists for the curious, for people who look at the ordinary and wonder what else it could become.

It is a place for design, technology, writing, culture, philosophy, experiments, and the random thoughts that appear somewhere between all of them.

Some ideas become projects. Some become stories. Some disappear. Others stay long enough to become something bigger.

02 / Mission

Make the future feel more alive.

To make sure the next generation gets to experience the beauty of youth and enjoy life.

01

Encourage curiosity.

Question the obvious. Explore different perspectives. Keep looking for what could exist beyond the familiar.

02

Create, don't just consume.

Turn ideas into projects, experiences, technology, culture, and things that can outlive the moment.

03

Make room for life.

Youth should contain discovery, friendship, creativity, adventure, mistakes, laughter, love, and ambition.

03 / Vision

Win in every timeline.

Build a life and body of work that remains meaningful regardless of how the future unfolds.

Every timeline is another possible future.

Different choices.
Different challenges.
Different opportunities.
Different versions of ourselves.

If the world changes, adapt. If the rules change, learn them. If one path closes, create another. If an idea fails, build again.

“Winning” is not defined solely by money, status, recognition, or conventional success. It means becoming capable enough to navigate uncertainty, creating things that matter, experiencing life fully, and leaving something behind that was worth building.

Become someone capable of thriving across all of them.
